How I work

Four rules behind every system here.

I work in English and 中文, both native, and I write strategy and content for both markets.

Report what happened.

"I tested it, here is what happened" teaches more than advice. A running system is the argument.

Receipts persuade.

A number, a screenshot, a before and an after. Without a receipt, it waits.

Failures are episodes.

What broke, the root cause, the fix and what it cost, published as readily as the wins.

Leverage over labour.

Anything done twice by hand becomes a system, so my hours go to strategy and judgement.
