How I work.

Four rules, applied to every system on this page.

Report, don't prescribe.

"I tested it, here's what happened" beats "you should." Observations from a real system, not advice from a podium.

Receipts persuade.

Numbers and screenshots, a before and an after. No receipt, no ship.

Failures are episodes.

What broke, the root cause, the fix, and what it cost. Published at the same rate as the wins.

Leverage over labor.

Anything done twice by hand becomes a system. The agent stack is the team; I stay on strategy and judgment.
