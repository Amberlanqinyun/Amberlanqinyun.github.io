How I work

Four rules I keep.

They shape every system on this page.

Report, don't prescribe.

"I tested it, here's what happened" beats "you should." A real system teaches more than a podium.

Receipts persuade.

Numbers and screenshots, a before and an after. No receipt, no ship.

Failures are episodes.

What broke, the root cause, the fix, and what it cost. Published at the same rate as the wins.

Leverage over labour.

Anything done twice by hand becomes a system. The agent stack is the team, and I stay on strategy and judgement.

Why Flow AI exists

I was the marketing team of one.

The system did the production. I did the judgement.

Read the full story

Ten years in marketing, across every part of it. Positioning and category work. Search, first on Google and now on ChatGPT too. Content calendars, brand voice, social, email, outbound, paid, events, analytics. In agencies and in house, for local operators through to enterprise software.

So I know a marketer's week. One person against a list written for five. The strategy sits in a document nobody has time to open. The content calendar slips, then slips again. Leads wait four days for a follow-up. The work outgrows the hours, and every quarter the list gets longer.

Three years ago I started building my way out. Agents with the strategy written into them. Content engines that hold a codified brand voice. Outbound lanes that research before they send. Search tooling that gets a business cited by AI assistants and ranked on Google.

Then I ran a whole marketing function on it at an AI company. One marketer, the full remit. Strategy, launches, content, thought leadership for three executives, events and reporting. The system did the production. I did the judgement. That is how one person held it.

Flow AI is that system, opened up. Every business owner I meet has the problem I had, with less time and no marketing background. Now the agents that ran my function run theirs.
