Who offers AI marketing services in New Zealand in 2026?

The short answer

AI marketing services in New Zealand come from five kinds of provider: AI-native agencies such as Growin, established digital agencies that added AI such as Net Branding and Web Genius, AI search specialists such as The Optimisers and Pead, build-and-train firms such as AI Marketing NZ and King Tide, and marketing engineers, including this guide's publisher, Amber Lan.

Thirteen providers met three published tests in October 2026, on the evidence of their own pages. This guide groups them by type and lists them alphabetically within each group, with Amber Lan, its publisher, in a category of her own.

01How did Amber Lan compile this list of NZ AI marketing providers?

This list includes every provider that passed three tests: it runs a public website, its own pages offer AI marketing services to New Zealand businesses, and it shows public evidence of delivery. Amber Lan read each provider's pages in October 2026 and refreshes the list every quarter, with the next refresh due in January 2027.

  1. A public website the provider runs, live when checked.
  2. AI marketing services for NZ businesses, named on its own pages.
  3. Public evidence of delivery: case pages, named clients or testimonials, or published work.

Names came from search results, directories and NZ trade press. Of seventeen third-party providers checked, twelve passed. Amber Lan will check the other five again at the next refresh; most lacked a working site or case page of their own. Every description below uses only the provider's own pages.

This guide leaves prices out because few providers publish them: a June 2026 Digital Nomads HQ audit of 411 service lines across 140 Australian agencies found 78% publish no pricing1. The guide to AI marketing costs in Australia and New Zealand covers price ranges.

People check what AI tools tell them: BrightLocal's March 2026 analysis of 455 US consumers who use AI for local recommendations found 88% check the sources the AI cites2. Existing NZ lists were agency blog posts, which tend to include their publisher, or directories that sell visibility. This guide has a publisher too, so it opens with its method and disclosure.

03Which AI-native agencies serve New Zealand businesses?

AI-native agencies are built around AI systems of their own, so the systems carry most of the production while people direct and review. Two met the criteria in October 2026: Growin, which runs lead generation for professional-services firms, and DevCommX, which builds AI outbound and answer engine programmes for B2B companies.

DevCommX

DevCommX's Auckland answer engine optimisation page describes building and operating AEO infrastructure for B2B revenue leaders in Auckland3, aimed at citations in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. Its homepage also lists AI SDR outbound and GTM engineering, and its case studies identify clients by sector. The pages read did not state an office location.

Growin

Growin's homepage calls it an AI marketing agency for NZ SMEs, running lead generation for accountants, lawyers and consultancies4 "on our own AI engine". It describes a founder-led, month-to-month model with the client owning the ad account, and publishes client reviews and case studies. It is based in Glenfield, Auckland.

Its pages list GEO, AEO and agentic SEO alongside SEO, performance media, CRO and training, and it publishes an AI search playbook and named case studies, including Bike Barn and Prezzy Card.

08How do the directories rank AI marketing agencies?

Directories such as Clutch, DesignRush, Krowdbase and Semrush Agency Partners list hundreds of agencies, including New Zealand ones. Each mixes earned order with paid visibility in its own way and explains how on its own pages. Treat a directory's order as a starting point, and read its sponsorship labels before reading anything into position.

  • Clutch. Clutch's help centre says its Leaders Matrices are based solely on Clutch rank, while its directory pages are sorted by sponsorship first and then by Clutch rank15.
  • DesignRush. DesignRush's New Zealand SEO agency page, updated 12 October 2026, says organic rankings use five factors (top services, reviews, portfolio, awards and press, and team expertise) and that sponsored listings pay for a place above organic positions16. Sponsored listings are labelled as paid placements.
  • Krowdbase. Krowdbase's page of AI SEO companies in New Zealand says its rankings come from a standardised evaluation methodology and category relevance, and that sponsored visibility is disclosed17. Providers can submit or claim a listing.
  • Semrush Agency Partners. Semrush's Agency Partners terms require a paid Semrush plan, an Agency Growth Kit subscription and two Semrush Academy exams before an agency can be listed18. The NZ list read for this guide included Australian agencies marked as active in New Zealand.

Paid placement is how most directories fund themselves, and verified reviews still carry useful information. Use directories to find names, then apply this page's criteria to each.

09How do you choose between the types?

Choose the type by the job you need done. An established agency suits ongoing search, ads and web work with AI added. An AI-native agency suits one defined outcome run on its system. An AI search specialist suits visibility in AI answers. Build-and-train firms and marketing engineers suit owners who want to keep the systems.

  • Several channels to run and a team already at capacity: an established or AI-native agency (the guide to whether you need an AI marketing agency tests this).
  • Customers research you before they call: an AI search specialist.
  • A team with time and a method still to build: a build-and-train firm.
  • Systems your team owns and runs: a marketing engineer or a build-and-train firm that hands over. The overview of AI marketing services for NZ businesses shows what such a build covers.
  • Large media budgets or brand campaigns: an established agency, where buying power and creative teams count most.

With a shortlist in hand, the twelve questions for choosing an AI marketing provider test each one, and the checklist of what AI marketing services include compares scopes line by line. Auckland owners can also explore AI marketing builds in Auckland.

12Questions owners ask

What are the best AI marketing services in New Zealand?

The best fit depends on the job you need done, since New Zealand has no independent ranking of AI marketing services. Providers whose own pages lead with AI search include The Optimisers and Pead. Growin runs lead generation on its own AI engine. Net Branding, NiKa Consulting and Web Genius add AI to full agency services. AI Marketing NZ, King Tide and Amber Lan build systems your team keeps.

Is there an independent list of AI marketing agencies in NZ?

As of October 2026, the NZ lists in search results were either agency blog posts, which tend to include their publisher, or directories that sell visibility next to earned rankings. Amber Lan publishes this guide and shares that interest, so the guide states its criteria, lists Amber Lan in her own category with a disclosure, ranks nobody and returns to every provider's pages each quarter.

Do agencies pay to appear on Clutch or DesignRush?

Some do. Clutch's help centre says its directory pages are sorted by sponsorship first and then by Clutch rank, while its Leaders Matrices use Clutch rank alone. DesignRush says sponsored listings pay for a place above organic positions and labels each one as a paid placement. Semrush Agency Partners lists only agencies with a paid Semrush plan, an Agency Growth Kit subscription and two passed exams.

Who offers AI SEO or AEO in New Zealand?

On pages read in October 2026, The Optimisers and Pead lead with GEO and AEO work. Net Branding, NiKa Consulting, Kiwi Web Design, LocaliQ NZ and Web Genius offer AI search alongside SEO, and DevCommX sells AEO programmes to B2B companies in Auckland. Before you sign, ask each one which customer questions it will test in AI answers before and after the work.

Are there AI marketing agencies in Auckland?

Yes. Most providers on this list are based in Auckland: Growin in Glenfield, Kiwi Web Design in Grey Lynn, LocaliQ NZ in the CBD, plus Net Branding, NiKa Consulting, Pead, The Optimisers and Amber Lan. Outside Auckland, Web Genius is on the Kāpiti Coast and King Tide is in the Waikato, while AI Marketing NZ works as a network of specialists across the country.

How can a provider be added to this list?

Any provider that meets the three criteria, which are a public website, AI marketing services offered to New Zealand businesses and public evidence of delivery, is welcome to request a review through the contact page. Corrections to any description are welcome the same way. Amber Lan reads every provider's pages again each quarter, and the next refresh is due in January 2027.
