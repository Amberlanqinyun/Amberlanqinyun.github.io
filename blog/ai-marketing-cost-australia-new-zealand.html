How much does AI marketing cost in Australia and New Zealand? (2026)

AI marketing cost depends less on the AI than on how you buy it: by the seat, by the hour, by the retainer, by the salary, or by the install. This guide puts all five options on one table in AUD and NZD, from published 2026 sources, so a small business can see what a month of shipped marketing actually costs.

The short answer

In 2026, AI marketing for a small business costs USD 20 to 99 a month per tool if you do the work yourself, NZD 60 to 150 an hour from a freelancer, NZD 1,500 to 10,000 a month from an agency, or NZD 90,000 to 130,000 a year for an in-house hire. AI marketing agents sit between the tool and the agency.

01Why AI marketing pricing is hard to compare

AI marketing cost is hard to compare because the five ways of buying it are priced on different units: software by the seat, freelancers by the hour, agencies by the monthly retainer, staff by the salary, and AI marketing agents by the install and the month. This guide puts all five on one table, in AUD and NZD, from published 2026 sources.

The market does not make it easy. In a June 2026 audit of 140 Australian agencies, Digital Nomads HQ found that 78% publish no prices at all1. New Zealand has fewer published guides again, and most of the ones that exist are written by agencies pricing their own work.

The reader here is the owner or marketing lead of a business with one to fifty staff, in Australia or New Zealand, who wants marketing to run every week without buying a whole department. Every figure below links to the page it came from, with the currency named. Where a number could not be sourced, the cell says so rather than guessing.

If the broader question is what AI marketing includes in the first place, the guide to AI marketing for small business covers the four systems and where to start. This page is only about the money.

02What does AI marketing cost in Australia and New Zealand?

In 2026, AI marketing costs USD 20 to 99 a month per tool done yourself, AUD 80 to 200 or NZD 60 to 150 an hour from a freelancer, AUD 800 to 6,000 or NZD 1,500 to 10,000 a month from an agency, and AUD 105,000 to 130,000 or NZD 90,000 to 130,000 a year for an in-house hire.

AI marketing agents and platforms sit between the tool and the agency. Most vendors bill in USD in both countries: Autohive lists USD 99 a month for its Starter plan and USD 499 for Team2, and HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional starts at USD 800 a month3 plus a required USD 3,000 onboarding fee. No New Zealand vendor publishes an AI marketing agent price in NZD.

Those ranges are wide because the options bundle different things. The rest of this guide takes them apart, one option at a time, so the comparison is between what each dollar buys rather than between headline numbers.

03What you are actually paying for

Every marketing price is a bundle of five things: hours of production, judgment about what to say and to whom, the software that does the work, the setup that connects it to the business, and accountability for results each month. The five options below bundle those differently, which is why comparing headline prices alone misleads.

  • Production hours. Writing, building, posting, researching prospects, fixing pages. This is the part AI has made cheap, and it is most of what an agency retainer or a junior salary used to buy.
  • Judgment. Deciding what the business should claim, which customers to go after, and what to stop doing. Still human, still expensive, and rarely more than a few hours a month for a small business.
  • Software. The tools that do the production. Cheap per seat, but an agency's tool stack is inside its fee and a freelancer's is often inside theirs. Do it yourself and it is a separate line.
  • Setup. Connecting the tools to the website, the CRM, the ad accounts and the analytics. One-off, often invoiced separately, and the source of most surprise costs.
  • Accountability. Who is responsible if nothing ships this month. A salary buys the most of this, a tool subscription buys none.

The useful question is not "what does it cost" but "what does one month of shipped marketing cost, and who carries the risk if it does not ship". Read the table with that question in mind.

05Option 1: do it yourself with AI tools

Doing it yourself costs USD 20 to 99 a month per tool and as many owner hours as the marketing needs. HubSpot's Marketing Hub Starter is USD 20 a seat, Jasper Pro is USD 69 a seat, and Autohive runs from a free tier through USD 20 and USD 99 plans. The cash cost is small; the time cost is the whole business.

The tools have become good enough that one person can draft a month of content, fix page titles and set up an email sequence in a weekend. What they cannot do is decide what to make, keep making it when the business gets busy, or tell the owner that the thing they built is not working. Those three jobs are the reason most self-run marketing stops by the third month.

There is also a tool sprawl cost. Digital Nomads HQ budgets AUD 300 to 800 a month in tool costs1 for a single in-house specialist, and a solo owner who tries to cover search, content, social and email ends up with a similar stack. Four subscriptions at USD 20 to 99 each is a modest agency fee with none of the accountability.

  • Works when: one channel matters, the owner enjoys the work, and revenue does not yet justify a fixed monthly fee.
  • Fails when: the owner is the bottleneck for production, or nobody reviews the output before it ships under the company's name.
  • Before spending anything: run the free AI-readiness benchmark to see which channel is actually broken.

06Option 2: a freelancer or AI marketing consultant

A freelance marketer costs AUD 80 to 200 an hour in Australia and NZD 60 to 150 an hour in New Zealand, with retainers from NZD 500 to 3,000 a month. An AI marketing consultant, who advises rather than produces, charges more per hour: NZD 150 to 250 in New Zealand, and AUD 191 an hour for CMO-level consultants in Australia.

The Australian figures come from two sources that agree. workspacein.com puts standard freelance roles at AUD 80 to 200 an hour5, with copywriters at AUD 80 to 180 and specialists at AUD 250 and up. Cemoh's live rate guide, updated 9 September 20266, averages AUD 813 a day across 23 marketing roles, from AUD 456 a day for a social media coordinator to AUD 1,451 a day for a CMO consultant.

In New Zealand, King Tide's July 2026 guide7 puts freelancers at NZD 60 to 150 an hour and consultants at NZD 150 to 250 an hour, or NZD 2,000 to 5,000 a month for ongoing advice. It also draws a floor: a business that cannot put NZD 1,000 to 1,500 a month into marketing should not hire anyone yet.

A freelancer who uses AI does not usually charge less per hour. They deliver more per hour, which is a real saving if the scope is fixed and a smaller one if it is open-ended. The structural cost is coordination: marketing needs several lanes, and a business with three freelancers has hired itself a part-time job managing them.

07Option 3: an AI marketing agent or platform

An AI marketing agent or platform costs USD 99 to 499 a month for self-serve products such as Autohive, USD 800 a month plus USD 3,000 onboarding for HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional, and AUD 800 to 2,000 a month where an Australian agency sells AI-led SEO to a small business. No vendor publishes an NZD price list.

An AI marketing agent is software that plans, produces and ships marketing work across several channels under a business owner's review, priced by the install and the month rather than by the hour.

The category is new enough that its pricing splits into three shapes. Self-serve platforms sell seats and credits: Autohive's published plans run Free, Lite at USD 20, Starter at USD 99 and Team at USD 499 a month2. Suites sell tiers with onboarding attached: HubSpot's Professional tier starts at USD 800 a month on an annual commitment with a required one-time USD 3,000 onboarding fee3, and Enterprise at USD 3,600 a month with USD 7,000 onboarding.

The third shape is an agency that runs AI inside a retainer. Marketix Digital's AI SEO pricing guide9 puts small businesses at AUD 800 to 2,000 a month, mid-sized companies at AUD 2,000 to 6,000, and a one-off AI SEO audit at AUD 1,000 to 3,500. It also notes that about 20% of such a budget goes to tools, with the rest split between content and strategy.

What the agent buys that a tool does not is the plan and the cadence: the work is scoped, scheduled and shipped without the owner having to start it each week. What it buys that an agency does not is a price set by the department installed rather than by the hours consumed. What it does not buy is a person in the room; the owner still reviews and decides. Flow AI's installable sprints are one example of this model, with search and AI search as the most common first department.

08Option 4: a marketing agency retainer

A conventional agency retainer runs AUD 2,000 to 6,000 a month for most Australian businesses, with full service at AUD 3,000 to 8,000 and a median small-business retainer of AUD 3,400 to 4,800. In New Zealand the range is NZD 1,000 to 10,000 a month, with multi-channel work at NZD 1,500 to 4,000 and full service from NZD 4,000.

Three Australian sources triangulate the same picture. Precision Digital's 2026 guide10 puts SEO retainers at AUD 1,500 to 5,000, Google Ads management at AUD 800 to 2,500 plus spend, and social media management at AUD 1,000 to 3,000 a month. Digital Nomads HQ's audit of 140 agencies1 found a median SEO price of AUD 1,470 a month and a typical Google Ads management range of AUD 1,200 to 3,500 plus spend. workspacein.com5 reports mid-market full-service engagements at AUD 8,500 to 14,500 a month.

New Zealand is cheaper at the bottom and similar at the top. Jelly Digital8 prices a single channel at NZD 500 to 1,500 a month, SEO at NZD 750 to 3,000, social at NZD 500 to 2,000, and Google Ads management at NZD 300 to 1,500 flat or 10 to 20% of spend. King Tide7 sees NZD 1,000 to 2,000 for one channel, NZD 2,000 to 4,000 for a primary channel plus support, and NZD 5,000 and up for multi-channel.

The structural issue with a small retainer is what it buys. At AUD 2,000 or NZD 1,500 a month, a business is buying a limited number of agency hours, and small accounts get the junior ones. The agencies now branding themselves as AI marketing agencies are addressing exactly this: the production hours drop, so the same retainer buys more output. The judgment hours, and the account management, cost what they always did.

09Option 5: an in-house hire

An in-house marketer costs AUD 105,000 to 130,000 a year all in for one specialist in Australia, and NZD 90,000 to 130,000 in salary for a marketing manager in New Zealand plus about 20% in overhead. SEEK's advertised averages for marketing managers in the marketing and communications industry are AUD 111,784 and NZD 106,322 respectively.

The advertised salary is the visible half. Digital Nomads HQ's all-in figure1 of AUD 105,000 to 130,000 is built from an AUD 85,000 to 100,000 salary, 12% superannuation and AUD 300 to 800 a month of tools, which lands at AUD 8,700 to 10,800 a month. workspacein.com5 puts mid-level in-house marketers at AUD 80,000 to 120,000 plus super and senior at AUD 130,000 to 180,000, and adds 30% for the fully loaded cost.

New Zealand runs a step lower in salary. King Tide7 puts a marketing coordinator at NZD 55,000 to 70,000 and a marketing manager at NZD 90,000 to 130,000, with about 20% on top in tools, training, recruitment and overhead. SEEK's September 2026 data12 shows NZD 117,500 as the advertised average in Auckland Central, against AUD 125,000 in Sydney11.

A hire is the only option that puts a person in the room full time, and for a business past 20 staff that often matters more than the cash. The trade-off is that one person, however good, cannot cover search, content, social, outbound and reporting by hand. The best-run marketing hires in 2026 spend part of their week running AI systems, which is the point at which the agent and the hire stop being alternatives and start being a pairing.

10The hidden costs: data, setup and your own time

Hidden marketing costs in Australia and New Zealand include setup fees of AUD 500 to 3,000, reporting fees of AUD 150 to 400 a month, tool subscriptions of AUD 300 to 800 a month, platform onboarding such as HubSpot's USD 3,000, GST on top of nearly every quote, and the owner's own hours preparing data and reviewing output, which no invoice records.

  • Setup and onboarding. One-off setup fees of AUD 500 to 3,0005 are common at Australian agencies; Digital Nomads HQ found ads setup fees of AUD 499 to 1,999 and onboarding from AUD 5501. HubSpot's required onboarding is USD 3,000 for Professional and USD 7,000 for Enterprise.
  • Reporting and tools. Some agencies bill reporting separately at AUD 150 to 400 a month5. Whoever runs the work, tools cost AUD 300 to 800 a month for one specialist's stack.
  • Media spend and its management fee. Ad budgets go to the platforms on top of every figure in this guide, and management is often charged at 10 to 20% of that spend8 rather than a flat fee.
  • Tax. Australian agency prices are almost always exclusive of 10% GST1. New Zealand quotes usually exclude 15% GST. USD software bills also move with the exchange rate.
  • Your data. Every AI system is only as good as the customer list, the website analytics and the brand facts it is given. Cleaning a CRM, fixing tracking and writing down what the business actually claims is unpaid owner time, and it is the first month of almost every engagement.
  • Your time. Doing it yourself costs the most hours, an agency the fewest, and an agent sits in between: a review session a week, plus decisions. Price that time at the owner's own hourly rate and the table above changes shape.

The free pricing model on this site exists for exactly this reason: it adds the hidden lines to the headline price so the comparison is between totals.

11Verdict: the right option by business size

For a solo operator, tools plus one freelancer is the cheapest working setup. For 5 to 20 staff, an AI marketing agent gives several channels a weekly cadence for less than one salary. For 20 to 50 staff, a marketing lead running an agent or an AI-led agency beats a conventional retainer on cost per shipped piece.

Solo and under 5 staff

Cash matters more than time until it does not. Start with USD 20 to 99 a month of tools on the one channel that brings customers, and buy a freelancer's hours for the piece that needs a specialist: NZD 60 to 150 or AUD 80 to 200 an hour, scoped tightly. The trigger to move up is the month the owner becomes the reason nothing shipped.

5 to 20 staff

This is where the agent model earns its place. The business needs search, content and follow-up running every week, cannot justify AUD 105,000 to 130,000 or NZD 90,000 to 130,000 a year for a hire, and would get junior hours from a small retainer. One department installed and run monthly, reviewed by the owner or an office lead, is the cheapest way to get several channels moving at once.

20 to 50 staff

Marketing now needs a full-time owner inside the company, so hire the lead. Then equip that person with an agent or an AI-led agency rather than a conventional retainer, because the production hours are the part worth automating. A lead at SEEK's advertised averages plus an agent costs less than a lead plus a full-service retainer of AUD 3,000 to 8,000 or NZD 4,000 to 10,000 a month, and ships more.

13The short version

AI marketing in Australia and New Zealand costs anywhere from USD 20 a month to NZD or AUD 130,000 a year, and the number that matters is the cost of one month of marketing that actually ships. Tools are cheapest in cash and dearest in hours; a hire is the reverse; an AI marketing agent is priced in the middle and built for the 5 to 50 staff business.

14Questions owners ask

What does AI marketing cost in New Zealand?

In New Zealand in 2026, a freelance marketer charges NZD 60 to 150 an hour or NZD 500 to 3,000 a month on retainer, agencies charge NZD 1,000 to 10,000 a month depending on how many channels they run, and a marketing manager costs NZD 90,000 to 130,000 a year in salary plus about 20% in overhead. AI marketing platforms bill in USD, typically USD 20 to 499 a month, and no New Zealand vendor publishes an AI marketing agent price in NZD.

Is an AI marketing agency cheaper than a normal agency?

At the small-business end, yes. Marketix Digital puts AI-led SEO retainers at AUD 800 to 2,000 a month for small businesses, against a median conventional small-business retainer of AUD 3,400 to 4,800 a month reported by workspacein.com. The saving comes from the production hours, not the strategy hours, so the gap narrows for mid-sized scopes where both models charge AUD 2,000 to 6,000 a month.

How much does an AI marketing consultant or freelancer charge?

In Australia, Cemoh's network of vetted marketing consultants averages AUD 813 a day or AUD 107 an hour as of September 2026, with CMO-level consultants at AUD 191 an hour. In New Zealand, King Tide reports freelancers at NZD 60 to 150 an hour and consultants at NZD 150 to 250 an hour, or NZD 2,000 to 5,000 a month for ongoing advice. A consultant who uses AI tools does not usually charge less per hour; they deliver more per hour.

Is it cheaper to hire a marketer or use an AI marketing agent?

An AI marketing agent is cheaper on cash. One in-house specialist in Australia costs AUD 105,000 to 130,000 a year all in, and a New Zealand marketing manager NZD 90,000 to 130,000 plus overhead, which is AUD 8,700 to 10,800 a month before the person does anything. An agent runs several channels for a fraction of that, but it still needs an owner or lead spending a few hours a week on review and decisions.

What hidden costs come with AI marketing?

Expect setup fees of AUD 500 to 3,000, reporting fees of AUD 150 to 400 a month at some agencies, tool subscriptions of AUD 300 to 800 a month, GST on top of almost every quoted price, and platform onboarding such as HubSpot's required USD 3,000 fee for Marketing Hub Professional. The largest hidden cost is the owner's time cleaning up customer data and reviewing output, which no invoice shows.
