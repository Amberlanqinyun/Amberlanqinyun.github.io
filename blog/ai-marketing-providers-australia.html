Who offers AI marketing services in Australia in 2026?

The short answer

In October 2026, Australian AI marketing services came from five kinds of provider: AI-native agencies such as Flowterra Labs, established agencies adding AI such as Yoghurt Digital, AI search specialists such as Prosperity Media, automation firms such as Bolder Digital, and remote build-and-handover practices. Few publish prices, so compare what each shows and what you keep.

This guide maps who offers AI marketing services in Australia on published criteria, in a field where most lists come from agencies ranking themselves or from directories that sell placement. It groups providers by type and describes each from its own pages, with no ranking.

01How did Flow AI build this list?

This list includes a provider only when it meets three published criteria, checked against its own website in October 2026. Each entry describes what the provider says it does, with a link to the page. The guide groups providers by type without ranking them, and refreshes the list every quarter.

  1. A public website that describes the service.
  2. AI marketing services offered to Australian businesses, stated on that site.
  3. Public evidence of delivery: named clients, case studies, testimonials or reviews.

Prices appear only where a provider publishes them, with the date read. Claims quoted are the providers' own; Flow AI has not independently verified them. Flow AI left out names from other lists when their own pages could not be read or showed no AI marketing service or delivery evidence.

An AI marketing provider is any business that sells marketing work in which AI systems do a meaningful share of the research, production, follow-up or reporting, whether it calls itself an agency, a consultancy, a software company or a sole practitioner.

Flow AI publishes this page and sits in its own category, and it describes itself with the same restraint as everyone else.

07What are directories and "best AI agency" lists worth?

Directories and ranked lists are good for finding names, and their order can reflect payment or the publisher's own interest. Each major directory explains its placement rules on its own pages, and several of the most shared Australian "best AI agency" lists are written by companies that place themselves near the top.

  • Digital Agency Network's Australian AI marketing page lists 20 agencies it says were vetted for portfolio, service, sector expertise and team transparency18. Its membership page listed paid tiers from USD 580 to 2,980 a year in October 2026, with more directory slots at higher tiers19.
  • Clutch's help centre says its directories are sorted by sponsorship and then by Clutch rank, while its Leaders Matrices use Clutch rank alone20, which weighs client reviews, work experience and market presence21.
  • DesignRush's Australian digital marketing page says sponsored listings pay for a placement above organic positions22, and that sponsorship does not change ratings or reviews.
  • The National AI Centre's AI Directory invites organisations to nominate themselves23, with no stated quality assessment.

Team 400, a Brisbane AI engineering consultancy, ranks itself first of five in its April 2026 list of top AI agencies in Australia24, on a page that does not mention marketing. Wireflow, an AI video software company, places itself second of six in its July 2026 list25, calling itself "the deliberate odd one out because it is not an agency". Use them for a long list, then check every name on its own pages.

08Which Australian rules must an AI marketing provider follow?

Any provider sending marketing emails or texts for an Australian business must follow the Spam Act 2003, which requires consent, sender identification and a working unsubscribe. If your business is covered by the Privacy Act, the Australian Privacy Principles also govern the customer data the provider's AI tools touch. Responsibility stays with your business, even when a provider presses send.

ACMA's spam guidance says you need consent from each recipient even when someone else sends your messages, and that unsubscribe requests must be honoured within 5 working days26. Messages must identify the business that authorised them. You stay responsible for consent on any list you buy, and address-harvesting software is banned, which matters for AI outbound tools that scrape contacts.

The OAIC lists 13 Australian Privacy Principles27. It says most small businesses with turnover of AUD 3 million or less are outside the Privacy Act, though health service providers and businesses trading in personal information are covered regardless28. Its October 2024 AI guidance recommends, as best practice, keeping personal information, particularly sensitive information, out of publicly available generative AI tools29. Ask every provider which AI tools will see your customer data and where they store it. The guide to AI marketing and privacy law in NZ and Australia goes further.

09How do you choose an AI marketing provider in Australia?

Start from the job you need done, then pick the type, then compare two or three names inside it. A business that wants ChatGPT to mention it should talk to an AI search specialist, while one that wants enquiries answered after hours is better served by an automation firm. Then ask each shortlisted provider for a written scope and price.

Prices are rarely public. Digital Nomads HQ's June 2026 audit of 411 service lines across 140 Australian agencies found 78% publish no pricing, with a median SEO entry price of AUD 1,470 a month30. In this list only Robotic Marketer publishes prices. The AI marketing cost guide for Australia and New Zealand sets out sourced ranges for tools, freelancers, agencies and hires.

In the first meeting, ask to see a system running on a real business, with the human review step, and ask which accounts, agents and data stay in your name if you leave. For large paid media budgets and brand campaigns, a full-service agency remains the right call. The twelve questions for choosing an AI marketing provider turn this into a scoring sheet, and the New Zealand edition of this list uses the same criteria.

12Questions owners ask

Who offers AI marketing services in Australia?

In October 2026, providers meeting this guide's criteria included the AI-native agencies Flowterra Labs (Sydney), AiMRed (Melbourne) and Due North (Brisbane); the established agencies Yoghurt Digital and Marketing Eye; the AI search specialists Prosperity Media, Optimising and Webmasters Group; the automation firms Bolder Digital and Robotic Marketer; and remote build-and-handover practices including Lilach Bullock and Flow AI, which publishes the list.

What is the best AI marketing service for a small business in Australia?

The best fit depends on the job you need done. To be mentioned in ChatGPT and Google's AI answers, start with an AI search specialist. To capture enquiries after hours, or to own systems your team keeps running, explore an automation firm or a build-and-handover practice. Whichever type you choose, ask to see a system running and get the scope and price in writing.

How much do AI marketing services cost in Australia?

Few providers publish prices. In this list only Robotic Marketer did, showing $99 a month for its software and $1,200 a month for a done-for-you service in October 2026, without naming the currency. Across the wider market, Digital Nomads HQ's June 2026 audit found 78% of 140 Australian agencies publish no pricing, with a median SEO entry price of AUD 1,470 a month.

Are "best AI agency in Australia" lists independent?

Many widely shared lists are published by companies that rank themselves, such as Team 400 placing itself first of five in April 2026. Directories such as Clutch and DesignRush state that paid sponsorship affects where agencies appear in their listings, while saying it does not change reviews or ratings. Use lists to find names, then check each provider's own pages and evidence.

Can an overseas provider run AI marketing for an Australian business?

Yes, and several do, working remotely from New Zealand and further afield. Australian rules still apply to the messages and the data. The Spam Act 2003 requires consent, sender identification and an unsubscribe honoured within five working days, and covered businesses must meet the Australian Privacy Principles. Your business stays responsible for consent even when a provider sends on its behalf.
