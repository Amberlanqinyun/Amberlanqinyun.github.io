The week

Most of a marketer's week is work about work.

Pulling the report. Reformatting the deck. Chasing the approval. Rewriting a brief that already exists.

The strategy sits unread. The calendar slips twice. A Tuesday lead hears back on Friday. The report gets rebuilt by hand from four tools.

Every marketer knows which hours matter: the positioning call, the buyer who is nearly ready. They get whatever is left.

The talent is already in the room. What it needs is capacity.

The layer

Flow Intelligence is the layer.

One place holds your positioning, and ten agents take their briefs from it. A marketing engineer reviews every send.

Your message is written in one place: Flow Intelligence. Every agent briefs from it, so the fiftieth piece holds the line of the first. Change your positioning, and every channel hears it that week.

The Flow Engine: ten agents, grouped by outcome. Get found: search, content, social. Get leads: outbound, conversion, launch, paid. Keep customers: community, measurement, reporting. Each installs in a fixed-scope, one-week sprint and comes to you documented.

Agents produce. A marketing engineer decides what ships. That one rule keeps this much marketing safe.

The rules

Four rules on every install.

  • 01Receipts persuade

    Numbers with their sources attached, or silence. Nothing on this site claims a result it cannot show.

  • 02A human on every send

    Agents draft. A marketing engineer reviews every piece before it reaches a customer.

  • 03You own all of it

    Every system is documented and handed over. Leave, and it keeps running.

  • 04Leverage over labour

    Anything done twice by hand becomes a system. People stay on strategy and judgement.
