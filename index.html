Questions

Good questions.

What is Flow AI?

Flow AI is an AI marketing transformation practice. It builds the agents that run a marketing function, starting from strategy. Its founder has ten years in marketing and three building agents. Flow AI builds each system with the team that will run it, and the work is open to inspect.

How is Flow AI different from a marketing agency?

Flow AI builds engineered systems with your team, led by one marketing engineer who reviews every send. An agency bills monthly for people: an account manager, a strategist, a writer and a media buyer. You agree the scope together up front, and everything Flow AI builds is documented and open to inspect.

How does a contract build start?

Every contract build starts with a written scope: the gap to close first, the system that closes it, and what your team keeps running after handover.

Who reviews the work before it goes out?

A marketing engineer reviews every send before it reaches a customer. Agents produce the work, and a person decides what ships. That review lets a small team run this much marketing and stand behind every send.

Where does Flow AI work?

Flow AI is based in Auckland, New Zealand, and works worldwide. Its published research covers Australia and New Zealand, with sourced pricing and adoption statistics. The systems run in any English-speaking market.

Is Flow AI an AI marketing agency?

Flow AI covers what people hire an AI marketing agency for: AI search, content, outbound and reporting. AI agents do the work, and one marketing engineer reviews every send. It works as a contract build with your team, and everything built is documented and handed over.

What is AI marketing transformation?

AI marketing transformation rebuilds a marketing function around AI agents. Agents take the recurring production: research, first drafts, scheduling, tracking and follow-up. People keep the judgement. Flow AI installs one agent per one-week sprint, and the whole Flow Engine then runs monthly.

Does Flow AI help marketers use AI?

Yes. Marketing leads use Flow AI to put AI agents to work in their own team. The agents take production, and the marketer keeps strategy and review. Every system is documented at handover, and the open-source skill library shows how each agent works.
