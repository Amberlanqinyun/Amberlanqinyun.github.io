The practical questions.

What is an AI marketing engineer?

An AI marketing engineer connects go-to-market strategy to the systems that execute it. The role combines positioning, search visibility, content operations, outbound automation, and measurement.

How is Flow AI different from a marketing agency?

You work directly with the practitioner who writes the strategy and builds the systems. There are no account-manager handoffs, and the work is designed to keep operating after launch.

How quickly can the first system go live?

The written go-to-market read arrives within the first week, and the first agreed system is designed to go live in week one.

Who is Flow AI for?

Early-stage teams and New Zealand businesses that need senior marketing thinking and hands-on execution without hiring a full internal function.

Who owns the systems Flow AI builds?

The client does. Code repositories, analytics accounts, content, and documentation are created in the client's name or handed over at launch. Cancelling the retainer never means losing the machine.

What is AI-search optimisation?

AI-search optimisation, also called answer engine optimisation (AEO) or generative engine optimisation (GEO), is the practice of structuring a website so AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity cite it when answering buyers' questions. Research presented at KDD 2024 by Aggarwal et al. found that adding quotations to a page improved its visibility in AI-generated answers by up to 41 percent, and adding statistics by up to 33 percent.
