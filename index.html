FAQ

Who is Amber Lan?

Amber Lan is an AI marketing engineer in Auckland, New Zealand. She builds the agents that run a marketing function, starting from strategy, with ten years in marketing and three building agents behind her. She builds each system with the team that will run it, and the work is open to inspect.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

An agency bills monthly for people: an account manager, a strategist, a writer and a media buyer. I design and build the systems that do that work, with your team, against a scope agreed up front. Everything is documented and open to inspect.

How does a contract build start?

Every contract build starts with a written scope: where the business is heading, the marketing function that will take it there, and the order in which each system goes live.

How is a build priced?

Each build is priced per project from the agreed written scope, so the full cost is known before work begins.

Who reviews the work before it goes out?

Every output is reviewed by a senior marketing engineer before release. Agents produce the work and people make the decisions, which lets a lean team run a full marketing function with confidence.

Where do you work?

I'm based in Auckland and work with businesses across New Zealand and Australia. Auckland teams can meet in person; everywhere else the build runs remotely on your own accounts. My published research covers both markets, with sourced pricing and adoption statistics.

Is this an AI marketing agency?

I cover what people hire an AI marketing agency for: AI search, content, outbound and reporting. The work runs as a contract build with your team, and every system is documented and handed over. Explore the AI marketing services.

What is AI marketing transformation?

AI marketing transformation rebuilds a marketing function around AI agents. Agents take the recurring production: research, first drafts, scheduling, tracking and follow-up. People keep the judgement. Each one-week sprint brings another system online, and the complete Flow Engine then runs monthly.

Do you help marketers use AI?

Yes. Marketing leads bring me in to put AI agents to work in their own team. The agents take production, and the marketer keeps strategy and review. Every system is documented at handover, and the open-source skill library shows how each agent works.
