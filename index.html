Questions.

What is Flow AI?

Flow AI is an AI marketing transformation practice: the strategy, the systems and the agents that run a marketing function, engineered end to end. It was built by a marketing engineer with ten years in marketing and three building the agents, and it runs marketing for businesses that would otherwise hire an agency or a marketer.

How is Flow AI different from a marketing agency?

An agency solves marketing with people: an account manager, a strategist, a writer and a media buyer, billed monthly. Flow AI solves it with engineered systems and one marketing engineer who reviews the work. You talk to the person doing it, the scope is fixed in advance, and everything built is documented and inspectable.

What is the free audit?

A free audit is a written assessment of your marketing, delivered within the week, with the gaps ranked and the first system to install named. It is free, needs no card and no call, and it is how every engagement with Flow AI starts.

Who reviews the work before it goes out?

A marketing engineer reviews every send before it reaches a customer. Agents produce the work; a human decides what ships. That review is the only way a small team can safely run this much marketing output.

Where does Flow AI work?

Flow AI is based in Auckland, New Zealand and works worldwide. Its published research covers Australia and New Zealand in depth, including sourced pricing and adoption statistics, and the systems themselves run for any English-speaking market.
