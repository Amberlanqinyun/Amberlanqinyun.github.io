The practice · one flywheel, five stages

Marketing as a flywheel, installed one stage at a time.

Flow runs marketing as one wheel with five stages: aim, get found, earn belief, open doors, learn. Each stage installs as a fixed-scope sprint in a week, and every turn of the wheel makes the next one cheaper.

The story: why a wheel.

Campaigns restart from zero every month. A flywheel keeps what every month earned, and spends it on the next turn.

The turn

Positioning aims the wheel. AI search makes you findable where buyers actually look, and the content engine makes what they find believable. Outbound opens doors those first stages warmed. Analytics shows which effort produced revenue and hands that back to the aim, so the next turn starts sharper than the last.

The compounding

A citation earned in month two is still answering buyers in month eight. A voice codified once never gets re-briefed. A reply from outbound teaches the claims register something the next campaign uses. Each turn costs less and returns more, and that is the whole design.

Underneath the five stages sit the eleven engineered systems from the home page. A sprint installs the stage that moves first for your business.

Install one stage at a time.

Each sprint is one stage of the wheel: a fixed deliverables checklist, a one-week clock and full handover, every time.

Stage 01 · Aim · one week

Positioning sprint

Start here if nobody has written down what you claim, to whom, and why anyone should believe it.

Claims register, one-page positioning, message architecture, and your first two channels.

Every later stage of the wheel pulls from this. See the fixed scope
Stage 02 · Get found · one week

AI-search sprint

Start here if buyers ask Google and ChatGPT about what you sell and you never come up.

AI-crawler access, llms.txt, structured data, citability fixes, and search submission.

Your claim becomes machine-readable, so Google and AI assistants cite you. See the fixed scope
Stage 03 · Earn belief · one week

Content engine sprint

Start here if content only ships when someone finds a spare afternoon.

Your brand voice codified as rules, a content engine configured, and your first month of content drafted through the quality gate.

Every piece joins a library that keeps selling long after it ships. See the fixed scope
Stage 04 · Open doors · one week

Outbound sprint

Start here if the pipeline runs on referrals and you cannot say where the next customer comes from.

A signal-based prospect list and one personalised sequence inside safe limits, with instant human handoff the moment a buyer replies.

Reaches buyers who were not looking yet, warmed by what they find when they check you. See the fixed scope
Stage 05 · Learn · one week

Analytics sprint

Start here if you cannot say which marketing effort produced last month's revenue.

Tracking installed and conversion events wired, with a one-page monthly report connected to your real numbers.

Shows which turn produced revenue and feeds it back into the aim. See the fixed scope
