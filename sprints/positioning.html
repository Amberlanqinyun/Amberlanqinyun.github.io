Questions.

What happens in the first week of the positioning sprint?

The free audit comes first: a written assessment of your positioning and message with the gaps ranked. Then the fixed-scope build ships inside the week, with every deliverable listed on this page and a documented handover at the end.

Do I need to hire anyone to run this afterwards?

No. The system is built to run on a monthly cadence with a human review, and the handover documents exactly how. If you would rather have it run for you, the monthly engine covers that.

How is this different from buying the same work from an agency?

You talk to the marketing engineer who does the work, the scope and the week are fixed before anything starts, and what gets built is documented and portable rather than held inside a retainer.
