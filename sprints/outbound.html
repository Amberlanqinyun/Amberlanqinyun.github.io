Questions.

What happens in the first week of the outbound sprint?

The free audit comes first: a written assessment of your outbound pipeline, gaps ranked. The fixed-scope build then ships every deliverable on this page inside the week. A documented handover closes it.

Do I need to hire anyone to run this afterwards?

No. The system runs monthly with a human review, and the handover documents exactly how. If you would rather Flow ran it, the monthly engine covers that.

How is this different from buying the same work from an agency?

You work with the marketing engineer who builds it, on a scope and week fixed upfront. Everything built is documented and portable, so it never sits inside a retainer.
