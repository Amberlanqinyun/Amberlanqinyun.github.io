Do you work with businesses outside Auckland?

Yes. Flow AI works with businesses anywhere in New Zealand. Builds run remotely on your own accounts, and Auckland businesses can also meet in person for the scoping workshop.

Is my customer data safe with AI marketing agents?

The written scope names exactly what each agent may read, and customer data stays inside the tools you already use. Agents draft from what they are allowed to see, and a person on your team approves anything that reaches a customer, in line with the Privacy Act 2020.
