Questions.

What is an AI agent developer?

An AI agent developer designs and builds software agents that use a language model to carry out a job, such as researching accounts or drafting content, with access to specific tools and data. The work covers the brief, data access, guardrails, review points, monitoring and documentation as well as the prompts.

What kind of AI agents does Amber Lan build?

Agents for marketing and sales work: AI search and SEO, content drafting in your voice, outbound research and first drafts, measurement and weekly reporting. Each agent is built for one defined job, and custom agents are scoped from the hours they give back.

Who owns the agents after the build?

Your team retains full ownership. Every agent, prompt, workflow and document is built on your accounts and handed over with written instructions, so the system can be run, changed or extended without Amber Lan.

How much does AI agent development cost?

Amber Lan prices each build per project once the written scope is agreed. Amber Lan's guide to AI agent development costs in New Zealand breaks the cost into sourced parts: people time, model usage, platform subscriptions and ongoing upkeep.

Do you work with businesses in Australia?

Yes. Amber Lan is based in Auckland and works with businesses across New Zealand and Australia. Builds run remotely on your own accounts, with calls scheduled across the two time zones, and Australian work follows the Spam Act 2003 and the Australian Privacy Principles.
