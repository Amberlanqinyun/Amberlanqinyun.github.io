Questions.

What are AI marketing services?

AI marketing services are marketing work delivered through AI systems, such as agents for search, content, outbound and reporting, with experienced marketers setting the direction. Some providers run the systems for you on a retainer. Amber Lan builds them with your team and hands them over as a working system.

Is Amber Lan an AI marketing agency?

Amber Lan does the work people look for when they search for an AI marketing agency: AI search, content, social, outbound and reporting. The model is a partnership. One marketing engineer builds the agents with your team, documents them and hands them over as a complete system.

Do you work with businesses in Australia?

Yes. Amber Lan is based in Auckland and works with businesses across New Zealand and Australia. Builds run remotely on your own accounts, with calls scheduled across the two time zones, and Australian work follows the Spam Act 2003 and the Australian Privacy Principles.

How much do AI marketing services cost?

Amber Lan prices each build per project once a written scope is agreed. Market ranges for AI tools, freelancers, agencies and in-house hires in New Zealand and Australia are set out, with sources, in Amber Lan's cost guide.

Who owns the agents after the build?

Your team retains full ownership. Every agent, prompt, workflow and document is built on your accounts and handed over with written instructions, so the system can be run, changed or extended without Amber Lan.

How is the work reviewed?

Review is built into every workflow. Each agent drafts, a named reviewer on your team approves the work before release, and the scope records who reviews what. Positioning, pricing, claims and spend remain human decisions.
