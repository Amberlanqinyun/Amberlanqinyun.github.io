Questions.

What does a GTM engineer do?

A GTM engineer builds the systems that feed sales: target account lists, enrichment, outbound sequences, CRM data flows and the reporting that shows which activity creates pipeline. A marketing engineer builds the systems that produce marketing. Amber Lan does both.

Is there a GTM engineer in New Zealand I can hire?

Yes. Amber Lan is a GTM engineer and AI marketing engineer based in Auckland, open to senior roles in New Zealand, Australia or remote teams, and to contract and fractional work.

What is the difference between a GTM engineer and RevOps?

RevOps runs the revenue process and its tools across marketing, sales and customer success. A GTM engineer builds and automates the parts that create pipeline: lists, enrichment, outbound and the data flows into the CRM. In a growing team one person often covers both.

Which tools does Amber Lan work with?

HubSpot, Apollo, HeyReach, n8n, Zapier, Claude Code and MCP, SQL on BigQuery, GA4 and Google Tag Manager. Every build runs on your own accounts, so the tools and the data stay yours.

Can a GTM engineer work fractionally?

Yes. A fractional engagement is a set number of days each month, spent running and extending the systems alongside your team. It suits companies that need the systems before they need a full-time seat.

How do I start?

For a role, send the job description or a short note. For a build, send a short brief on your pipeline and the tools you use. Amber replies personally within two working days.
