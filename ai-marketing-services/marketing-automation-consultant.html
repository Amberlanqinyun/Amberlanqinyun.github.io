Questions.

What does a marketing automation consultant do?

A marketing automation consultant designs and builds the workflows that move leads and content through a business without manual handling: capturing enquiries, routing them, sending consent-based follow-up, keeping the CRM current and reporting results. With AI added, agents draft the messages and summaries inside each workflow.

Do you work with HubSpot?

Yes. Amber has architected HubSpot from scratch and works with it alongside Apollo, HeyReach, n8n and Zapier. If your business runs a different CRM, the written scope sets out how the workflows connect to it before the build begins.

Marketing automation or AI agents: which do I need?

Most teams benefit from both. Automation moves data and triggers steps on a schedule, and AI agents handle the drafting and research inside those steps. The build starts with the simplest automation that closes the gap, and adds an agent where drafting or research takes the most time.

Does the automation follow NZ spam law?

Yes. Every commercial email follows the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007: consent, clear identification of the sender and a working unsubscribe. Australian contacts follow the Spam Act 2003. A named reviewer on your team approves the first message to every new contact.

How much does marketing automation consulting cost?

Amber Lan prices each build per project, in NZD plus GST, once the written scope is agreed. Platform subscriptions are paid directly by your business, and Amber Lan's tool comparison sets out what n8n, Zapier and Make charge.
