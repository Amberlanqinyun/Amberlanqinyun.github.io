Questions.

What does an AI automation consultant do?

An AI automation consultant finds the repetitive work in a business, decides which parts AI can draft or move safely, and builds the workflows that do it, with a person approving anything that reaches a customer. At Flow AI that work centres on marketing and sales: enquiries, content, CRM upkeep, outbound research and reporting.

Is Flow AI an AI automation agency?

Flow AI does the work people look for when they search for an AI automation agency in Auckland. The model is a partnership. One engineer maps the work with your team, builds the automations on your accounts and hands them over with documentation, so the workflows stay in your business.

Which tools do you build with?

Usually the ones you already pay for. The working stack includes HubSpot, n8n, Zapier, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Claude Code with MCP connectors. The written scope names each tool, what it may read and who owns the account.

How much does an AI automation consultant cost in NZ?

Flow AI prices each build per project, in NZD plus GST, once the written scope is agreed. For market context, the Flow AI guides to AI consultant rates and AI agent development costs set out sourced ranges for New Zealand and Australia.

Can we meet in person in Auckland?

Yes. Flow AI is based in Auckland, so the mapping workshop and the handover can happen at your office. Between sessions the build runs on your own accounts, and your team reviews as it goes.
