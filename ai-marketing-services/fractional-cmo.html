What a fractional CMO covers

The whole marketing function, led and running.

Strategy at the top, a system of agents underneath, one plan holding both.

  • Positioning and strategy

    Who you serve, what you say and the claims you can prove, written down as the brief every piece of work answers to.

  • Plan and budget

    A marketing plan with priorities, spend and targets agreed with the owner, reviewed each quarter.

  • AI search and content

    Search, AI answers and a monthly content engine, run by agents and reviewed before anything ships.

  • Outbound and demand

    The buyers worth reaching, researched and contacted personally, with every reply handed to a person.

  • Team and agencies

    Briefs and reviews for your team, freelancers and agencies, so every piece of work serves one plan.

  • Reporting

    A monthly report to the owner or board: what ran, what it produced and what changes next.

Questions.

What does a fractional CMO do?

A fractional CMO is a senior marketing leader who works for your business part of each month: setting positioning and the plan, directing the team and agencies, and reporting results to the owner or board. Amber Lan also builds the AI agent system that carries the plan out, so the strategy keeps running between her days.

How much does a fractional CMO cost in New Zealand?

It depends on the days each month and the scope. As a market reference, Robert Walters' 2026 guide puts Auckland contract marketing managers at NZD 70 to 105 an hour. Amber Lan's fractional guide sets out the sourced ranges; her own engagements are agreed as set days each month after a written plan.

What is the difference between a fractional CMO and an AI CMO?

An AI CMO is software that plans marketing. A fractional CMO is a person who is accountable for it. Amber Lan works as the person and builds the AI systems underneath, so a business gets the judgement and the capacity together.

Do you work in person in Auckland?

Yes. Amber Lan is based in Auckland and runs planning workshops on site. The rest of the work happens on your own accounts, with a shared weekly review.

Can you work with our existing agency?

Yes. Agencies keep production-heavy work such as paid media and brand campaigns. The fractional CMO writes the brief, reviews the output and holds everyone to one plan.
