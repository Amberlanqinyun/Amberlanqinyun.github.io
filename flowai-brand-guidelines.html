The Flow AI brand system.

One source of truth for how Flow AI looks, sounds, and holds its position. Every surface that carries the name passes through here: site, socials, proposals, reports, directory profiles.

01

Foundation: the entity and the claims.

The entity string · use verbatim everywhere

Canonical entity
Flow AI (flowai.co.nz) · Amber Lan, AI marketing engineer · Auckland, New Zealand

Search and AI engines resolve brands by name, and "Flow" is contested in NZ marketing: Flow Digital (flow.co.nz), Flow Marketing NZ, and Discflow's FLOWAI product all exist. The full entity string, name plus domain plus person plus place, is what keeps citations resolving to us. Use it in every bio, directory profile, and byline. Never "Flow" alone.

The claims

Category line · leads every surface
You run the business. Flow AI runs the marketing.
The offer, one breath
Your marketing, run by an AI system built and operated by one engineer. NZ$1,000 a month, cancel anytime, first report in 14 days.
The person
I run marketing solo at an AI startup, on an agent stack I built myself. Flow AI is the same method, pointed at your business.

Category vocabulary we are claiming: AI marketing engineer, marketing engineering, growth engineering, answer engine optimization (AEO). Use these terms consistently; they're unclaimed in NZ and consistency is what claims them.

02

The marks.

Flow AI.
Flow AI.
  • The flow-lines symbol: three lines converging into one. Always stroke, never filled; cream on ink or ink on cream.
  • The wordmark is "Flow AI." with the full stop, set in the brand face at weight 600. "Flow AI" without the stop mid-sentence. Never "FlowAI", "flowai", or "Flow".
  • Favicon: the "F." tile, ink square, cream letter.
  • No shadows, glows, gradients on the marks. No rotation, no stretching, no recolouring.
03

Colour: warm ink and cream, nothing else.

Warm ink#0E0E0D · canvas, text on light
Panel#171715 · cards on ink
Cream#FAF8F3 · text on ink, light surfaces
Dimcream at 58% · body text on ink
  • No pure black (#000) and no pure white (#FFF); both read cold. The warmth is the system.
  • No accent colour, no gradients, no glassmorphism, no neon. Hierarchy comes from brightness and scale.
  • Film grain at 4% is permitted on the site canvas only.
04

Typography: one face, weight does the work.

  • Face: Google Sans Flex (self-hosted variable), falling back to the system sans stack.
  • Weights: 300 for ledes and large quiet text, 400 body, 500 sub-heads and values, 600 headlines and the wordmark. 700 only at display sizes.
  • Tracking: negative on headlines (−0.02 to −0.045em, tighter as size grows); wide uppercase tracking (0.16 to 0.22em) reserved for labels.
  • Case: sentence case everywhere. No title case, no all-caps headlines. Labels may be uppercase.
  • Never a second typeface, never decorative type effects.
05

Voice: the practitioner, receipts first.

Flow AI speaks as one person who does the work, never as a faceless firm. Four rules govern every sentence, on every surface:

  • Report, don't prescribe. "I tested it, here's what happened" beats "you should."
  • Receipts persuade, adjectives get cut. A number and a screenshot beat any superlative. No receipt, no claim.
  • Failures are episodes. What broke, the cause, the fix, the cost. Published at the same rate as wins.
  • Honest pricing is voice. Numbers appear in plain text, in NZD, with the comparison anchors. We never make the reader ask.

Never claim

Expert, guru, authority, No.1, guaranteed results, "the right way", client outcomes that don't exist yet. Founding-client honesty is a feature: "no client logos yet, on purpose" is on-brand; an invented case study is a fireable offence.

07

Before anything ships.

  • Entity string correct and complete where identity appears.
  • Every claim carries a receipt or a link to one. No invented results, no borrowed numbers.
  • Prices current: $997 sprint, $1,000/month retainer, $500 referral. One source of truth, this file and the site.
