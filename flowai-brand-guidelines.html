The Flow AI brand system.

Three inks, one face, centered stacks and air. Every surface that carries the name passes through here.

Three inks. Nothing else.

Every colour on a Flow AI surface is one of these three at some density.

#0e0e0d · --bg, --ink

Amber

One job per screen: the filled button, or the single result value in a tool.

Two surfaces sit one and two steps above the ground for inputs and image plates: --surface #171715 and --surface-strong #1d1c19. The only Amber wash is --accent-soft, Amber at 14 percent, and it is the ceiling for any gradient on the site.

Warm, never pure.

The substrate reads as printed paper and ink, not a screen.

  • No pure black, no pure white. #000 and #fff read cold. The warmth is the system.
  • No fourth hex. Hover states change density, not hue: a brightness filter or an alpha step of the same ink. Never a lighter Amber or a third dark.
  • Grain is allowed, up to 4 percent. The site runs a fixed film grain at 2.5 percent. Grain is texture, not an ink.
  • Washes are the ceiling. A 12 to 16 percent Amber wash on one card is the most gradient a page may carry. No glows, no mesh, no glass.
  • Photographs are printed. Prefer halftone assets in the inks. A live full-colour image is printed into the page with filter: grayscale(1) invert(0.93) contrast(0.92), so Cream, not white, is its brightest value.

Proof

The work is visible.

Strategy is only credible when you can inspect the systems beneath it.

Work with me See the work
  • The stack. .stack spaces its children: 1.5rem after an eyebrow, 1.75rem before a lead, 2.5rem before a button row. Never hand-tune these gaps.
  • The air. Sections take --section, clamp(6rem, 13vw, 11rem), top and bottom. A hairline separates neighbours. A hero carries at most four text elements.
  • The width. .wrap is 1080px with 24px gutters; reading columns use .narrow at 44rem. Content grids sit below the stack and align left.
  • One eyebrow per three sections. The label is rare on purpose. If two adjacent sections want one, neither gets it.

What the page refuses.

The system is mostly a list of things that never appear.

  • No icons. No icon fonts, no inline SVG marks, no emoji. A step is a number in .small; a result is a large figure in the display type, not a gauge.
  • No cards, boxes, shadows or gradients. Hairlines at --hair do all the separating. The one exception is a live pattern frame like the demo above.
  • One label per intent. Every link to the contact page reads Work with me. A logo wall is logos alone.
  • Printed images only. Halftone assets in the inks, or a full-colour image run through the print filter. Never a white slab on the dark page.
  • No dashes in copy. Commas, colons and full stops carry the rhythm. Currency reads as AUD or NZD with the figure, when a price is shown at all.
  • Entity string intact. Flow AI (flowai.co.nz), Amber Lan, AI marketing engineer, Auckland, New Zealand. Never "Flow" alone, never "FlowAI".

Before a page ships

The audit.

Run every line. A page that fails one does not ship.

  1. Count hexes outside the three inks. The answer is zero, with #171715 and #1d1c19 as the two allowed surfaces.
  2. Look for #fff, #000, and any full-colour image that has not been printed.
  3. Confirm one Amber job per viewport: the filled button or the result value, never both.
  4. Count eyebrows above section headlines. At most one for every three sections.
  5. List every contact-intent label. Each one reads Work with me.
  6. Check :active on every .btn and the Amber focus ring on every focusable element.
  7. Search visible text for em and en dashes, title case headings, and any uppercase outside .eyebrow.
  8. Count inline <svg> elements. Only a live chart may remain.
