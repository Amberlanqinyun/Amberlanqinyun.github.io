Which Australian rules must an AI marketing provider follow?

Any provider sending marketing emails or texts for an Australian business must follow the Spam Act 2003, which requires consent, sender identification and a working unsubscribe. If your business is covered by the Privacy Act, the Australian Privacy Principles also govern the customer data the provider's AI tools touch. Responsibility stays with your business, even when a provider presses send.

ACMA's spam guidance says you need consent from each recipient even when someone else sends your messages, and that unsubscribe requests must be honoured within 5 working days. Messages must identify the business that authorised them. You stay responsible for consent on any list you buy, and address-harvesting software is banned, which matters for AI outbound tools that scrape contacts.

The OAIC lists 13 Australian Privacy Principles. It says most small businesses with turnover of AUD 3 million or less are outside the Privacy Act, though health service providers and businesses trading in personal information are covered regardless. Its October 2024 AI guidance recommends, as best practice, keeping personal information, particularly sensitive information, out of publicly available generative AI tools. Ask every provider which AI tools will see your customer data and where they store it. The guide to AI marketing and privacy law in NZ and Australia goes further.

How do you choose an AI marketing provider in Australia?

Start from the job you need done, then pick the type, then compare two or three names inside it. A business that wants ChatGPT to mention it should talk to an AI search specialist, while one that wants enquiries answered after hours is better served by an automation firm. Then ask each shortlisted provider for a written scope and price.

Prices are rarely public. Digital Nomads HQ's June 2026 audit of 411 service lines across 140 Australian agencies found 78% publish no pricing, with a median SEO entry price of AUD 1,470 a month. In this list only Robotic Marketer publishes prices. The AI marketing cost guide for Australia and New Zealand sets out sourced ranges for tools, freelancers, agencies and hires.

In the first meeting, ask to see a system running on a real business, with the human review step, and ask which accounts, agents and data stay in your name if you leave. For large paid media budgets and brand campaigns, a full-service agency remains the right call.
