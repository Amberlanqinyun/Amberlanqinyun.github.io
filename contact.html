Questions.

What is an AI marketing engineer?

An AI marketing engineer connects go-to-market strategy to the systems that execute it. The role combines positioning, search visibility, content operations, outbound automation, and measurement.

How is Flow AI different from a marketing agency?

You work directly with the practitioner who writes the strategy and builds the systems. There are no account-manager handoffs, and the work is designed to keep operating after launch.

How quickly can the first system go live?

The written audit arrives within the first week, and the first agreed system is designed to go live in week one.

Who is Flow AI for?

Early-stage teams and small businesses in Australia and New Zealand that need senior marketing thinking and hands-on execution without hiring a full internal function.

What does AI marketing cost in Australia and New Zealand?

Flow AI prices by system: a fixed-scope one-week install, then a monthly run that can be cancelled at any time. Current figures come with the free audit, so you see the number against your own situation rather than a rate card.

What is AI-search optimisation?

AI-search optimisation, also called answer engine optimisation (AEO) or generative engine optimisation (GEO), is the practice of structuring a website so AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity cite it when answering buyers' questions. Research presented at KDD 2024 by Aggarwal et al. found generative engine optimisation lifted visibility by up to 40 percent, with the largest gains going to pages that rank fifth rather than first.
