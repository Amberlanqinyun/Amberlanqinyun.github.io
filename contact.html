FAQ

What is an AI marketing engineer?

An AI marketing engineer connects go-to-market strategy to the systems that run it. The work spans positioning, search visibility, content operations, outbound automation and measurement.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

You work side by side with the person who writes the strategy and builds the systems, with no account manager in between. Decisions move at the speed of the work.

How quickly can the first system go live?

I send the written scope within a few days of our first conversation, and a typical build goes live in its first week.

Who is this for?

It suits teams that want an AI marketing system built alongside them, from early-stage startups to established in-house marketing teams. For hiring managers, the same work serves as Amber Lan's portfolio.

What does AI marketing cost in Australia and New Zealand?

I scope and price each build per project. A typical build is a fixed-scope week, with the price set in the written plan before work begins.

What is AI-search optimisation?

AI-search optimisation helps AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity cite your site in answers. It is also called answer engine optimisation (AEO) or generative engine optimisation (GEO). Research presented at KDD 2024 by Aggarwal et al. found GEO lifted visibility by up to 40 percent. The largest gains went to pages ranking fifth rather than first.
