How it runs

Step by step, then a person checks it.

  1. 01
    Read the websiteHomepage, about, pricing, product, blog and customer pages: what you do in your own words, the tagline, features, pricing and proof.
  2. 02
    Search the webThird-party descriptions, reviews, comparisons and company records. How others describe a business is often clearer than its own copy.
  3. 03
    Name the competitorsThree to five direct competitors, each with where it overlaps and where it differs.
  4. 04
    Write the briefOne self-contained Brand DNA file. Anything not public is marked as not found, and every source is listed with the date it was read.
  5. 05
    A marketing engineer reviews itAmber checks the result before it reaches you. That rule holds on every Flow AI run.

In and out

What it needs, and what you get back.

What it needs

What comes back

  • What the business does, in plain words
  • Target customer, pricing and key features
  • Three to five competitors and your differentiators
  • Brand voice and social proof
  • Content gaps, with why each one matters
  • Every source, dated
