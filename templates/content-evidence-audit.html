How it runs

Step by step, then a person checks it.

  1. 01
    Load the contextThe draft, plus your Brand DNA if one exists, which becomes the source of truth for claims about your business.
  2. 02
    Pull out every claimLinks, statistics, named sources, research citations and claims about your own product.
  3. 03
    Open every linkEach one is fetched and read to confirm it supports the exact claim attached to it. Every link, with no sampling.
  4. 04
    Trace every numberStatistics are traced to their original source, and the patterns of invented figures are flagged.
  5. 05
    Check your own claimsMetrics and features are checked against your Brand DNA, and dates and numbers against each other.
  6. 06
    A marketing engineer reviews itAmber checks the result before it reaches you. That rule holds on every Flow AI run.

In and out

What it needs, and what you get back.

What it needs

  • A public page link, or a shared draft link
  • Your Brand DNA, if you have one

What comes back

  • A result for every claim: passed, broken, mismatched or unverifiable
  • Critical issues to fix before publishing
  • Warnings worth fixing
  • A specific fix for each issue
  • What passed, so you know it is safe
