Before you install.

What is an AI agent skill?

An AI agent skill is a reusable SKILL.md file that gives a compatible agent specialised instructions, workflows, references, and quality checks for a defined job.

Who builds these skills?

Seventeen skills are built and maintained by Amber Lan at Flow AI. Forty-one are growth playbooks by Iris Wei of Gingiris, fifty are marketing skills by Corey Haines, and one is the Humanizer editing skill by Siqi Chen. All the curated ones are MIT licensed and install from their authors' own repositories. Every card names its author.

Can I inspect a skill before installing it?

Yes. Every entry links directly to its public source on GitHub. Read the instructions, dependencies, and scripts before you add it to an agent.

Will these work in every AI agent?

They use the SKILL.md format. Exact installation and tool support depend on the agent and CLI you use. The GEO suite is currently documented for Claude Code.

Are all listed skills equally portable?

No. The Flow AI research and audit skills are portable and self-contained. The GEO suite and Latticework brief are designed to be installed. The Novie content engine depends on its documented brand files and local workflow. The Gingiris playbooks are self-contained SKILL.md files installed from their own repositories.
