Flow AI · the dot

The amber dot opens its eyes.

The Flow AI mark is four dots rising to one amber dot. The amber dot is Flow Intelligence, and it is not a thing: it is a light, a core of intelligence with two eyes where the light is absent. The cream dots are the agents it sends out, and they are solid, because agents do tangible work. This page is the character, its physics, and the engine that runs it on this site, open source like everything else here.

Physics

Why it feels alive now, and did not before.

Round two moved the eyes with a linear ease, which is how a cursor moves. Living things move in saccades and springs. Eight changes, all small, all borrowed from animation fundamentals rather than invented.

  1. Springs, not eases. The gaze is a damped spring (stiffness 140, damping 18), so it overshoots a hair and settles, like an eye.
  2. Saccades. Left alone, it picks a new point every 1.5 to 4 seconds and jumps there. Eyes never drift.
  3. Eyes lead, body follows. The body tilts toward the gaze a beat later, through a slower spring. That lag is what reads as weight.
  4. Perspective. Looking right, the right eye grows 6 percent and the left shrinks 12. A flat disc becomes a head.
  5. Anticipation. A blink widens the eyes 4 percent for 60 ms before it closes. Closes in 70, opens in 140. Nothing in nature is symmetrical.
  6. Eyes as absence, with a glint. On the light core the eyes are the two places the light is not, and each carries one cream point in the upper left: the light reflected in the eye. Solid agent dots have the same point. It is the cheapest cuteness there is, and it is why the eyes read as wet and round.
  7. Squash and stretch. Breathing stretches along the vertical by 1.2 percent; the nod squashes to 90 and recovers through the same spring.
  8. Looking down narrows the eyes. A 15 percent lid when the gaze drops, so reading looks like reading.

Unmistakable

Eyes make it a face. Behaviour makes it yours.

Anyone can draw a dot with eyes. The behaviours come from what Flow AI actually does: reading sites, briefing agents, waiting for a human, working overnight in Auckland.

01 · the double blink and the trail

A signature blink, and the trail it leaves.

Humans blink once. The dot blinks twice, fast, like a camera confirming focus. And whenever it moves, the three dots of the mark appear behind it and fade: the character carries the logo wherever it goes.

02 · the reading scan

It reads, line by line.

While working, the eyes sweep left to right and drop a line, as a person reads. This is what an AI-search audit looks like from the outside.

03 · the shy glance

It looks at you, looks away, looks back.

Put your pointer on it: it tilts its head, meets your eye, glances shyly away, and comes back, with a little amber in its cheeks. Focus the field and it looks at it; type and it follows the caret.

04 · the nod

Approval is a nod.

When a run is approved, the body dips once and the eyes squint. No tick mark, no confetti. A nod from a colleague.

05 · auckland time

It keeps your hours.

Between ten at night and six in the morning in Auckland the site's dot is in the dark state. Visitors on the other side of the world see that it works while you sleep.

06 · eyes made of dots

The eyes are the trail.

The two cream dots nearest the amber one become the eyes when the trail folds in. Logo and face are the same four dots.

The mark and the pixel grid

The trail stays abstract. The character appears at 32 pixels and up.

Below 32 pixels the eyes are two smudges, so the header and footer keep the trail exactly as it is today. The favicon is the one exception: a single amber dot with eyes, drawn on the 16-pixel grid by hand rather than scaled.

The favicon, drawn on the grid.

A six-pixel-radius amber core with a soft edge, two two-by-two ink squares one pixel above the centre line, two pixels apart. Scaling the big face down produces grey mush; placing squares on the grid produces eyes that are crisp in every tab bar. The 32-pixel version uses the same drawing doubled, which is why it also stays crisp.

Ship as favicon.svg with shape-rendering="crispEdges" on the eyes, plus a 32 and 180 PNG for Safari and iOS.

Identifying the agents

Ten agents, ten cream dots, each with its own eyes.

Colour is off the table (three inks), so identity lives in the eyes. Each agent owns a style chosen for its job, so the Recents list reads without labels after a week. Hover any agent: it looks at you, then away, the way a colleague acknowledges you.

Option A from round two (one style, ten geometries) is kept in the API as a fallback for agents added later: FlowDot.mount(el, {agent: 'new-agent'}) derives a stable face from the name.

    Rules
    
    
    What keeps it cute instead of corporate-mascot.
    
  
    
  
    
    
    Always
      
      
    • Two eyes, nothing else. No mouth, no arms, no eyebrows, no hat. Expression comes from eye shape, direction and blink timing.
      • 
      
    • Light and matter. Flow Intelligence is a luminous amber core, the one glow the brand permits, because it is the one thing on the site that is not an object. Agents are solid cream. Eyes are ink, or the absence of light.
      • 
      
    • Asymmetric eyes. The right eye is 20 percent larger. Symmetry reads as an icon; the imbalance reads as a face.
      • 
      
    • The double blink, every 3 to 6 seconds. Never on a visible loop.
      • 
      
    • Springs and saccades. Nothing glides. Eyes jump, bodies settle.
      • 
    
    
    
    Never
      
      
    • No eyes under 32 pixels, except the hand-drawn favicon.
      • 
      
    • No second character. Agents differ by eyes, not by colour, clothes or body shape.
      • 
      
    • No talking. It never gets speech bubbles. The thread in the demo window is the product's, not the character's.
      • 
      
    • No sad state. Failure is flat eyes and a tilt, not tears.
      • 
      
    • No motion for its own sake. Every animation marks a real state: working, waiting, done, away.
