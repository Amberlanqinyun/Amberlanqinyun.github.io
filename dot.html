Physics

What makes it feel alive.

Living things move in springs and saccades, and glass shows itself where it catches the light. Eight rules, all small, borrowed from animation fundamentals and from how glass is lit.

  1. Springs, not eases. The gaze is a damped spring (stiffness 140, damping 18), so it overshoots a hair and settles, like an eye.
  2. Saccades. Left alone, it picks a new point every 1.5 to 4 seconds and jumps there. Eyes never drift.
  3. Eyes lead, body follows. The face shifts toward the gaze first; the body tilts after it through a slower spring. That lag is what reads as weight.
  4. Eyes that open for you. At rest the eyes are calm closed arcs. When you arrive, or there is work to watch, they open into a white eye with a big round pupil that rolls toward you, then settle closed again.
  5. Perspective. Looking right, the right eye shrinks 6 percent and the left grows 3. A flat shape becomes a head.
  6. The blink is a lid. A lid comes down from the top in 70 ms and lifts in 140, twice. At the bottom of the blink a calm arc stands in for the closed eye.
  7. Lit, never outlined. No strokes around bodies or eyes. The glass is translucent, its top edge catches the light, a soft highlight sits up top and warm light pools at the bottom. Flow Intelligence is the same glass with the amber light inside.
  8. Squash and stretch. Breathing stretches along the vertical by 1.2 percent; the nod squashes to 90 and recovers through the same spring.

Eye studio

Same glass, same physics. Calm arcs at rest, open eyes for attentive moments, a flat line for exact ones. Every open eye is the same size and every line, mouth included, the same weight, so the family reads as one.

On this site every face rests with calm arcs and opens its eyes when something needs its attention; each agent wears the set that fits its job.

Unmistakable

Eyes make it a face. Behaviour makes it yours.

Anyone can draw a dot with eyes. The behaviours come from what the system actually does: reading sites, briefing agents, waiting for a human, working overnight in Auckland.

01 · the double blink and the trail

A signature blink, and the trail it leaves.

Humans blink once. The dot blinks twice, fast, like a camera confirming focus. And whenever it moves, the three dots of the mark appear behind it and fade: the character carries the logo wherever it goes.

02 · the reading scan

It reads, line by line.

While working, the eyes sweep left to right and drop a line, as a person reads. This is what an AI-search audit looks like from the outside.

03 · the shy glance

It looks at you, looks away, looks back.

Put your pointer on it: it tilts its head, meets your eye, glances shyly away, and comes back, with a little amber in its cheeks. Focus the field and it looks at it; type and it follows the caret.

04 · the nod

Approval is a nod.

When a run is approved, the body dips once and the eyes smile. It reads as a nod from a colleague, with no tick mark or confetti.

05 · auckland time

It keeps your hours.

Between ten at night and six in the morning in Auckland the site's dot is in the dark state. Visitors on the other side of the world see that it works while you sleep.

06 · where the mark came from

The first mark was a trail.

The first mark was four dots rising to one amber dot. Fold the trail in and the two cream dots nearest the amber one become eyes. The family mark grew out of that face.

The mark and the pixel grid

The mark is the family.

The mark is the whole system in one cluster: Flow Intelligence in front, and behind it one agent from each team, Get found, Get leads and Keep customers. It holds as one shape down to 20 pixels. The favicon is the orb on its own, edge to edge, with eyes drawn larger so they read in a tab.

The favicon: the orb, edge to edge.

No tile and no dark border, only the glass orb on a transparent ground, so it sits cleanly on any tab colour. The eyes are drawn larger than the character's so they still read at 16 pixels; from 32 up the smile shows too.

Identifying the agents

Ten agents, ten glass shapes, each with its own face.

Colour stays off the table, so identity lives in shape and face. Each agent's body and eyes come from its job, so the Recents list reads without labels after a week. Hover any agent: it looks at you, then away, the way a colleague acknowledges you.

Agents added later wear a glass circle with the standard face until they earn a shape: FlowDot.mount(el, {agent: 'new-agent'}).

    Spec and API

    API

    <script src="/assets/dot.js" defer></script>

// place a character with markup alone
<svg class="fdot" data-fdot="search" viewBox="0 0 100 100"></svg>   // or data-fdot="flow"

// Flow Intelligence, in the demo window status
var fi = FlowDot.mount(document.querySelector('#status svg'), { role: 'flow' });
fi.set('review');          // idle · think · work · review · done · late · error
fi.lookAt(0.2, -0.1);      // −1..1, overrides saccades until lookAt(null)
fi.nod();                  // on approve
fi.blink(true);            // double

// an agent, in Recents. Shape and face come from the agent map;
// agents the map does not know get a glass circle with the standard face.
FlowDot.mount(li.querySelector('svg'), { role: 'agent', agent: 'search' });

// the dark state follows Auckland time on its own
FlowDot.autoNight(fi);     // 22:00-06:00 Pacific/Auckland

// every mounted dot shares one requestAnimationFrame loop and
// pauses off screen. Nothing is hidden if the script fails:
// the SVG you mount can carry a static dot as its fallback.

    Rules
    
    
    What keeps it warm and distinct from a corporate mascot.
    
  
    
  
    
    
    Always
      
      
    • Two eyes and a mouth, nothing else. No arms, no eyebrows, no hat. Expression comes from the eyes, the mouth and the timing.
      • 
      
    • One material. Flow Intelligence and every agent are the same glass. Flow Intelligence carries the amber light inside; the agents carry it as warm light pooling at the bottom.
      • 
      
    • Lit, never outlined. No strokes around bodies or eyes. Edges show only where they catch the light.
      • 
      
    • One eye rule. Every open eye is the same size: a white eye and a flat ink pupil, no shine. Every line eye and every mouth share one weight.
      • 
      
    • Soft everywhere. Every corner is round. Springs and saccades: nothing glides, and the double blink comes every 3 to 6 seconds.
      • 
    
    
    
    Never
      
      
    • No glass under 56 pixels. Small agents turn solid cream so the face still reads.
      • 
      
    • No second colour. Agents differ by shape and face, never by colour or clothes.
      • 
      
    • No talking. It never gets speech bubbles. The thread in the demo window is the product's, not the character's.
      • 
      
    • No sad state. Failure reads as a dazed look and a wobbly mouth.
      • 
      
    • No motion for its own sake. Every animation marks a real state: working, waiting, done, away.
